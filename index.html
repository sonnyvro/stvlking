about.
Information regarding me.

stvlking is an alias of sonnyvro — a dark trap & cinematic producer working out of the east coast since 2024. The sound sits somewhere between late-night sample loops, horror-score tension, and the low-end weight of modern trap.

The project started as bedroom experiments and grew into a catalog of beats, songs, and pure art. Every release is built around the same idea: atmosphere first, everything else second. Just trying to relate lol.

For beats, leases, features, or placements — reach out via any of the links on the right.
